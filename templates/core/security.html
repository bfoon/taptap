What exactly is switched on
  • DDoS protection: TCP SYN cookies; invalid packets dropped; a source opening more than 100 connections to the router at once is blocked for 1 hour.
  • IDS/IPS: a port scan of the router blocks the scanner for a day; the 4th login attempt within about a minute on WinBox, SSH, API, Telnet or FTP blocks the source for 1 hour; hotspot clients that port-scan or flood mail servers (spam bots) are blocked for 1 hour.
  • Detections are logged on the router as “TapTap DDoS” / “TapTap IPS”. MikroTik cannot inspect packet contents (there is no Suricata/Snort on RouterOS): this is pattern-based firewall protection.
  • Locked yourself out from WinBox after mistyping the password? Use Unblock all here, or wait an hour.
