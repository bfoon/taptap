How access works

  • The invitation must be accepted by the other business owner.
  • Access is created as a dedicated Collaborator membership, not Admin.
  • Team, Settings, Subscription and destructive warning controls are never offered through collaboration.
  • TapTap's existing business switcher keeps each business's data isolated.
  • Either owner can end the collaboration and remove cross-business access.
