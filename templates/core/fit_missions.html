🎯 Missions — Fit does it later, by itself

A mission waits for its moment — a time, a timetable, the quietest moment of the night, or something that happens — then does its job and tells the people you chose. Easiest: just ask Fit, e.g. “Tonight between 01:00 and 05:00, restart Mast when fewer than 3 people are online, and email Awa when it is back.”
