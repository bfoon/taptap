Two ways to put a page on your router

Offline on the router
Export gives you login.html and alogin.html. Upload them to the router’s hotspot folder. Works even when the internet is down.
Hosted by TapTap
The router forwards customers to your TapTap page. Edits go live instantly and codes are checked before login. Needs one walled-garden rule.
