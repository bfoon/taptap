How customers see it

On your routers (recommended)
Publish or Make default installs the page in each router’s hotspot folder, so phones see it the moment they join — before they have Internet. TapTap re-installs it when you edit the page, your plans or your branding.
Manual
Prefer WinBox? Use Export for router in a page’s menu and drag the files into the hotspot folder.

On your routers

The default login, “connected” and status pages are served by each router, so customers see your voucher page before they get Internet.

Loading…

Routers download the pages from TapTap over HTTPS using a link that expires in 2 hours. A walled-garden entry for TapTap is added so device identification and adverts work before login. Routers on TapTap Link install at their next check-in.
