Questions

Good to know

Is it safe to install from outside the Play Store?

Yes — it is the same app, built by TapTap and published here. Android shows the warning for every app that does not come from the Play Store.{% if app.release.sha256 %} The file's SHA-256 is {{ app.release.sha256 }}.{% endif %}

Does it cost anything?

The app is free. It shows what your TapTap plan includes — the same as the website.

Which phones work?

{{ app.min_android }}. On iPhone, add TapTap to your Home Screen from Safari until the iPhone app is ready.

Can my staff use it?

Yes. Each person signs in with their own team account and sees what their role allows — cashiers sell, viewers look.

How do I update it?

Download it again from this page and tap Install: it replaces the old one and you stay signed in.

Will it ring when the app is closed?

Yes, when notifications are on: customer calls, team calls, sales, complaints and messages arrive like any other app's.
