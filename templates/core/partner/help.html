How selling as an agent works

  1. Find Wi-Fi near you and apply. The owner accepts you and decides your discount — e.g. 20 % means you pay D2,000 for vouchers customers pay D2,500 for.
  2. Top up any amount. The owner can accept it or lower it.
  3. Pay cash, Wave, QMoney or Afrimoney. With the owner's Wave set up for automatic payments, your batch comes the second you pay; otherwise when the owner confirms.
  4. Sell each voucher at the shop price: send it by WhatsApp or SMS, or print the batch. Mark what you sold to see your margin.
  5. The vouchers run on the owner's Wi-Fi. If a customer has a problem, open your voucher checker from that owner's page.
{% else %}

How reselling works

  1. Add your wallets (Wave, QMoney, Afrimoney) under Wallets & shop — customers pay you there.
  2. Browse the Market: each batch shows its network's grade, uptime, daily users and how many batches are ahead of you.
  3. Reserve and pay the seller. The batch is yours until its last voucher is used.
  4. Share your shop link. Customers on that Wi-Fi who buy online are also served from your batch, in turn.
  5. Confirm customer payments that are not automatic — the customer gets the voucher at once.
