Speed steps

Each step: after this much data in the period, the voucher’s speed drops to this. Add more steps to slow it further. Speeds are per device, in Mb/s (0.5 = 500 kb/s).

What a customer experiences

  • Checked at every live sync (every minute or two). A customer slowed down sees it at once — no need to log in again.
  • Speed caps sit only on connected devices and are removed when they log off, so an IP address given to someone else is never slowed.
  • From a voucher’s page you can give one customer full speed back until the period resets.
